2005 Songhu Road, Shanghai, China

College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University

I’m Yuxuan Lin, currently a Ph.D. student at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University, where I was advised by Prof. Wenqiang Zhang. I am enrolled in an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. program and expect to graduate in 2029. Before that, I received my bachelor’s degree from East China University of Science and Technology in 2024. I completed the master’s stage in 2026 advised by Prof. Wenqiang Zhang.

My research focuses on Computer Vision, Multimodal Learning, and Embodied AI.
